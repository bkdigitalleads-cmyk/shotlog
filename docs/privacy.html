Shot Log

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Shot Log ("the app", on the App Store as Peptide Tracker: GLP-1 Shots) is a log for injections on a schedule, for iPhone. It is made by Brian Kershner and built so that your health information stays on your device.

What you log

Your medications, their doses and schedules, the shots you log with their time, dose and site, your weight and waist, how you feel, protein and water counters, notes, goals and the name you may put on a report are saved only in the app's storage on your iPhone. They are not uploaded, synced or sent to us or anyone else. The app has no account system and no server, and it works offline. It does not use your location, the camera, your photos, contacts or Apple Health.

Reports and exports

The PDF report and the CSV file are created on your iPhone and go only where you choose to send them (Mail, Messages, Files, AirDrop, a printer and so on).

Purchases

If you buy Shot Log Pro, the purchase is processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat, a purchase-management service, to confirm purchases. RevenueCat receives a random anonymous identifier and your purchase history for this app, never your name or anything you logged. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Notifications

If you turn reminders on, the app schedules local notifications on your iPhone for the days your shots are due, and one more an hour later if you tap Remind me in an hour. The notification does not name your medication. If you start a free trial and allow notifications, it also schedules one reminder the day before the trial ends. All of these are created on the device. No push service is used and nothing is sent from a server.

Analytics and tracking

The app contains no analytics, no ads and no tracking. We do not collect usage data. On the App Store the app's privacy label lists Purchases, not linked to you, because RevenueCat keeps the purchase history described above.

Deleting your data

Delete any shot or entry from History, use Settings, Delete everything, or delete the app. Everything it stored goes with it.

Not medical advice

Shot Log is a personal log. It does not diagnose, treat, calculate doses or give medical advice. Follow your prescriber about your medication and your doses.
