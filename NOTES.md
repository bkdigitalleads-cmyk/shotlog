# Shot Log change log

Newest first. One entry per commit: date, version/build, what changed and why, and how it was checked.

- 2026-11-14: 1.0.0 build 1, part 2 of 7: logging (branch claude/shotlog-1.0.0, no build).
  - Today (src/screens/Today.tsx): one screen. The next-shot leaf (the notebook's margin rule down its left) says "Due Thursday" with "In 5 days, Nov 19" under it, the medication and its current dose, and a small body map with the last site (ink dot) and the next one in the person's rotation (blue ring), and the line "Last: left abdomen. Next in your rotation: right abdomen." Under it one large Log shot button (the blue, 64 pt, the pen glyph, the dose and next site as its second line). With a stack (Pro), the other medications are rows under the leaf, each with its own due line and Log. Then "Also today": Weight (last, goal) and How I feel (today's entries, or how many days since the last shot), and the protein and water counters.
  - Log sheet (src/screens/LogShot.tsx): opens filled in with the medication due soonest, its dose on that day (src/schedule.ts doseOn, so a backfilled shot from before a dose change gets the old dose), the next site already picked, and "Now"; Save is the only tap needed. Everything can change: the medication (Pro stack), amount and unit, the site on the map (tap the picked one again to leave it empty), "Earlier" opens the date and time, a note. Editing a shot from History offers Delete (asks first). A shot entered more than an hour after it was taken is marked "Added later".
  - Body map (src/ui/BodyMap.tsx): front and back figures drawn as one smooth ink line each (points through a Catmull-Rom curve, so it reads hand drawn, not clip art), eight sites: abdomen, thighs, upper arms on the front, glutes on the back, left and right as the person's own ("Left and right are your own."). Marks: dotted ring, ink dot for last, blue ring for next, ink disc with a check for picked; history mode shades by count. Every site is a button with a VoiceOver label ("Right abdomen, next in your rotation"). Targets are as close to 44 pt as the drawing allows without overlapping (about 31 pt on a 393 pt phone; VoiceOver users get the labels).
  - Rotation (src/sites.ts nextSite): the site that followed the last site the previous time it was used, else the next among the sites used so far in first-use order, else the map's order. Neutral words only.
  - My medications (src/screens/Meds.tsx): name (free text; Semaglutide, Tirzepatide, Liraglutide, Dulaglutide and Retatrutide offered as tags while typing, generic names only), dose as amount plus unit (mg, mcg, mL, units, typed by the person), schedule (every N days with a stepper, or chosen weekdays), the first shot date, and a reminder switch. "Change the dose" logs a new dose from a date; the dose history lists each change with its date. Removing a medication keeps its shots (they keep their own copy of the name and dose). One medication free; "Add another medication" opens the paywall for free users.
  - Weight (src/screens/Entries.tsx WeightTray): quick entry with the last weight as the placeholder, optional waist (in or cm, following the unit), Now or Earlier, the goal shown and set in place. How I feel (FeelTray): the eight chips (nausea, constipation, heartburn, fatigue, headache, low appetite, site reaction, other), several at once, mild, moderate or strong, Now or Earlier, a note; the title line says how many days since the last shot.
  - Protein and water: tap-to-add counters on Today (+10 g, +25 g; +8 and +16 fl oz with lb, +250 and +500 mL with kg; touch and hold takes one off, after asking), against goals the person sets in Settings; none are suggested. Water is stored in mL and shown in fl oz with lb, so a unit switch keeps the numbers right. Pro: free users see the counters with Pro and a tap opens the paywall.
  - History (src/screens/History.tsx): every shot, weight and entry by day, newest first, filters (All, Shots, Weight, Feel); tap any to edit or delete; the plus adds a past shot, weight or entry with the date open. Free shows the last 30 days and says how many older entries Pro keeps.
  - Reminders (src/reminder.ts): asked once, right after the first saved shot ("Remind you when the next one is due?", Turn on reminders or Not now); "Shot due today" on each active medication's due day at the chosen time (9 am unless changed in Settings), rescheduled whenever shots, medications, Pro or settings change; nothing for a day already past. The notification has "Remind me in an hour"; it opens the app, which schedules the snooze and says when. The body is the same for every medication ("Log it in Shot Log when you are ready."), so the lock screen never shows a medication name. Free: one active medication, so one reminder.
  - Settings (src/screens/Settings.tsx): Pro (part 4), my medications, reminders and their time, lb or kg (the goal converts with it), weight, protein and water goals, protein and water on Today, what you are tracking, the name on the report, delete everything, email support (bkdigitalleads@gmail.com), help, privacy, terms, About with the medical line, the version (seven taps in test builds).
  - Rating trigger secondShot: after a save once two or more shots are logged (src/flow.ts guard).
  - Insights and Report tabs are titles only until part 3.
  - Checked: npx tsc --noEmit clean; npm test 15 pass (4 new: weights by day and the weekly change, change since the first shot, side effects by days since the shot, protein and water); npx expo export --platform ios bundles.
